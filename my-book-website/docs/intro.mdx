# Introduction

Welcome to AI-Native Driven Development, a comprehensive guide to building applications that are designed from the ground up with artificial intelligence at their core.

This book explores the fundamental concepts, architectural patterns, and practical implementations that define AI-native applications. We'll cover everything from infrastructure and model development to deployment and monitoring.

## What You'll Learn

- How to design AI-native architectures that scale
- Infrastructure patterns for AI workloads
- Model development and deployment strategies
- Building AI-enabled user interfaces
- Monitoring and evolving AI systems

Let's begin our journey into the world of AI-native development.
