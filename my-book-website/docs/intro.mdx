# Course Syllabus: Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics

Welcome to Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics, a comprehensive 13-week course on building systems that bridge digital AI with physical reality.

This course explores the fundamental concepts, tools, and practical implementations that define Physical AI - AI models that understand and interact with the physical world through robotic systems. We'll cover everything from robotic operating systems to humanoid development and conversational robotics.

## Course Structure

This 13-week intensive course provides a comprehensive understanding of Physical AI through theoretical foundations and practical implementations:

### Weeks 1-2: Introduction to Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence
- Foundations of Physical AI and embodied intelligence
- Transitioning from digital AI to robots that understand physical laws
- Overview of humanoid robotics landscape
- Sensor systems: LIDAR, cameras, IMUs, force/torque sensors
- Physical AI principles and constitutional requirements

### Weeks 3-5: ROS 2 Fundamentals
- Core ROS 2 architecture and concepts
- Nodes, topics, services, and actions
- Building ROS 2 packages with Python
- Launch files and parameter management
- ROS 2 core concepts and design patterns

### Weeks 6-7: Robot Simulation
- Gazebo simulation environment setup
- Robot description: URDF and SDF formats
- Physics simulation and sensor simulation
- Unity for robot simulation and visualization
- Simulation best practices and validation

### Weeks 8-10: NVIDIA Isaac Platform
- Isaac SDK components and architecture
- AI-powered perception and manipulation
- Reinforcement learning for robot control
- Sim-to-real transfer techniques
- Isaac Sim for advanced robotics simulation

### Weeks 11-12: Humanoid Robot Development
- Humanoid robot kinematics and dynamics
- Bipedal locomotion and balance control
- Manipulation and grasping with humanoid hands
- Natural human-robot interaction
- Advanced control strategies for humanoid systems

### Week 13: Conversational Robotics
- GPT model integration and natural language processing
- Speech recognition and natural language understanding
- Multi-modal interaction: speech, gesture, vision
- Conversation design principles
- Building conversational interfaces for robots

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:
- Understand and apply Physical AI principles and embodied intelligence concepts
- Build and deploy systems using ROS 2 (Robot Operating System)
- Create and validate robot simulations with Gazebo and Unity
- Develop AI-powered robotic applications using NVIDIA Isaac platform
- Design and implement control systems for humanoid robots
- Integrate conversational AI models for natural human-robot interaction

Let's begin our journey into the world of Physical AI.
