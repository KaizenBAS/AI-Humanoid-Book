# Training and Fine-tuning

Training and fine-tuning are fundamental processes in AI-Native development that determine the capabilities and performance of models.

## Training Approaches

### Supervised Learning

Supervised learning uses labeled datasets to train models:

- Classification models for categorical outputs
- Regression models for numerical predictions
- Quality of training data directly affects model performance

### Unsupervised Learning

Unsupervised learning discovers patterns in unlabeled data:

- Clustering algorithms group similar data points
- Anomaly detection identifies unusual patterns
- Dimensionality reduction simplifies complex data

### Reinforcement Learning

Reinforcement learning trains agents through trial and error:

- Reward systems guide learning behavior
- Applications in game playing and robotics
- Long-term strategy optimization

## Fine-tuning Strategies

Fine-tuning adapts pre-trained models for specific tasks:

### Transfer Learning

- Leverage knowledge from general domains
- Requires less data and computational resources
- Faster deployment of specialized models

### Domain Adaptation

- Adjust models for specific industry contexts
- Account for domain-specific language and concepts
- Preserve general capabilities while specializing

### Few-shot Learning

- Adapt with minimal task-specific examples
- Leverages pre-trained model knowledge
- Effective for specialized applications with limited data

## Training Optimization

### Data Efficiency

- Data augmentation techniques
- Active learning for optimal label selection
- Synthetic data generation

### Computational Efficiency

- Distributed training across multiple devices
- Mixed precision training
- Gradient compression for communication efficiency

### Model Performance

- Hyperparameter optimization
- Regularization to prevent overfitting
- Cross-validation for performance assessment
